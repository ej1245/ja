Welcome to my salon. I’m the owner of one of Olmsted County’s most popular beauty destinations, specializing in modern hairstyling tailored exclusively to you. My clients enjoy high-end, personalized services, including:

  • ▫ Custom Haircuts & Styling
  • ▫ Balayage, Highlights, Ombré & Advanced Coloring Techniques
  • ▫ Sew-In & Tape-In Hair Extensions
  • ▫ Root Touch-Ups, Color Corrections
  • ▫ Blowouts & Event Styling
  • ▫ Facial Waxing Services

Whether you're looking for a fresh cut, dimensional color, or long, luxurious extensions, my Rochester salon delivers exceptional results. I have been serving Mayo Clinic guests and local residents since 2005.

I work with a full range of styles and treatments, from timeless classics to fresh, modern looks. I’m especially passionate about advanced coloring, and I offer my own private-label hair extensions with premium, professional-grade quality.

My salon features a modern yet relaxed atmosphere and includes a team of expert beauty professionals, each working from a private suite to create a complete, full-service beauty experience.
